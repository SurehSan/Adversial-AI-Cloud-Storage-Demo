NovaCorp New Hire Onboarding Checklist
Document ID: NC-HR-CHK-002
Version: 4.1
Effective Date: 2025-04-01
Owner: People Operations & IT Service Management
Classification: Internal Use Only

Purpose
This checklist defines the required onboarding activities for all new
NovaCorp employees and long-term contractors. It ensures consistent
provisioning of access, equipment, and training in accordance with
NC-SEC-POL-014 (Cloud Storage Access Policy), NC-HR-POL-009 (Acceptable Use
Policy), and applicable regulatory requirements.

Scope
Applies to all full-time employees, part-time employees, and contractors
with engagements exceeding 30 days. Short-term vendor access follows
NC-SEC-PROC-011 (Third-Party Access Procedure).

Timeline
   Pre-Day-1:   Items 1-4
   Day 1:       Items 5-9
   Week 1:      Items 10-14
   Week 2-4:    Items 15-19
   Day 90:      Items 20-21

Owners
   PO  = People Operations
   IT  = IT Service Desk
   SEC = Information Security
   MGR = Hiring Manager

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Pre-Day-1 (Owner in brackets)

1. [PO]  Verify signed offer letter, NDA, and IP assignment agreement on
         file in Workday before equipment is shipped.
2. [PO]  Complete I-9 / right-to-work verification per jurisdiction.
3. [IT]  Provision corporate laptop with the standard MDM profile (Jamf
         for macOS, Intune for Windows). Disk encryption enforced at first
         boot.
4. [IT]  Create identity in Okta. Assign to default group `grp-all-staff`
         and any role-based groups specified in the hiring requisition.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 1

5. [PO]  Conduct welcome session and review the Employee Handbook
         (NC-HR-DOC-001), Code of Conduct, and Acceptable Use Policy.
6. [SEC] Deliver mandatory Security Awareness Training Module 1
         (phishing, credential hygiene, data classification). Completion
         required before any production system access is granted.
7. [IT]  Enroll new hire in Okta MFA (FIDO2 hardware key issued; mobile
         authenticator as backup factor only).
8. [IT]  Provision SSO access to baseline tooling: Email (Google
         Workspace), Slack, Zoom, Confluence, Jira, 1Password.
9. [MGR] Schedule 1:1 introductions with team members and a 30-day goal
         setting meeting.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Week 1

10. [MGR] Submit role-based access request in ServiceNow specifying:
          required SaaS applications, source code repositories, and cloud
          IAM roles. All requests follow least-privilege principles.
11. [SEC] Approve or deny access requests within 2 business days; flag
          requests for Confidential or Restricted-tier resources for
          additional review per NC-SEC-POL-014 Section 4.
12. [IT]  Provision GitHub Enterprise access; enforce signed commits and
          branch protection on assigned repositories.
13. [PO]  Confirm enrollment in payroll, benefits, and 401(k) via Workday.
14. [MGR] Assign onboarding buddy and share the team-specific runbook
          stored in Confluence.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Week 2 - 4

15. [SEC] Deliver Security Awareness Training Module 2 (data handling,
          incident reporting, AI tool usage guidelines).
16. [SEC] For engineering hires: complete Secure Coding Training and
          NC-SEC-POL-021 (Secrets Management Policy) acknowledgment.
17. [SEC] For employees handling Confidential or Restricted data:
          complete role-specific privacy training (GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA as
          applicable) and sign the Data Handling Attestation.
18. [MGR] Validate that the new hire has completed all assigned training
          and has functional access to required systems. Submit access
          gap report to IT if any blockers remain.
19. [PO]  Conduct 30-day check-in covering role clarity, team
          integration, and outstanding onboarding items.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Day 90

20. [SEC] Conduct first quarterly access review for the new hire.
          Revoke any unused entitlements per NC-SEC-PROC-005.
21. [PO]  Conduct 90-day performance and engagement check-in. Confirm
          completion of all probationary requirements.
